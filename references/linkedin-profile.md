# LinkedIn profile optimization checklist

Use this reference to audit or rewrite a person's supplied LinkedIn profile. Work from pasted or exported profile text unless an authorized read tool is available.

## Positioning brief

Establish the profile owner's target audience, desired opportunity, current role, credible differentiators, proof, constraints, and preferred voice. Do not invent employers, dates, qualifications, client names, results, or recommendations.

## Review by section

| Section | Job | Review questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | Make relevance clear in limited space | Does it identify the work, audience or domain, and credible distinction without keyword stuffing? |
| About | Connect expertise, point of view, proof, and next step | Is the opening specific? Are proof and claims sourced? Is the first person voice consistent? |
| Experience | Show scope and outcomes in context | Are responsibilities separated from results? Are dates, numbers, and role boundaries verified? |
| Featured | Provide evidence for the desired opportunity | Does each item reinforce positioning, and is it current and owned by the user? |
| Skills and supporting sections | Improve factual completeness | Are the most relevant verified skills and credentials represented without padding? |
| Visual and contact fields | Support recognition and action | Are recommendations framed as checks for the user, not claims that unseen visuals were reviewed? |

## Deliverable rules

Return current-state observations, priority changes, ready-to-paste copy, facts to verify, and optional tests. Preserve character-sensitive sections concisely but do not state current LinkedIn field limits unless they have been checked against an authoritative current source. Profile edits remain proposals; never claim to have applied them without a connected tool and explicit authorization.
