# Content quality guidance

## A useful draft

- Start with the audience's actual problem, not a generic claim.
- Make one clear point per post; support it with a concrete example, observation, or instruction.
- Adapt structure and length to the requested platform rather than pasting the same copy everywhere.
- Preserve the user's approved facts, voice, and calls to action. Mark assumptions clearly.
- Offer variants only when they serve a defined test, such as hook angle or call to action.

## A useful review

- Separate factual accuracy, clarity, relevance, and publishing readiness.
- Do not present reach or engagement predictions as facts.
- For analytics, distinguish what the data shows from a hypothesis about why it happened.
- For replies, match the context and avoid promises that the organization cannot keep.
