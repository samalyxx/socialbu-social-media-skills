# LinkedIn engager analytics

Explain what the available evidence supports and what to test next. Read [LinkedIn measurement](../../references/linkedin-measurement.md). Use [LinkedIn content planning](../../references/linkedin-content-planning.md) when turning findings into future briefs.

## Intake

Establish the profile or page, date range, comparison period, business objective, content scope, metric definitions, export or authorized data source, known paid distribution, campaign events, tracking gaps, and the decision this review should inform.

Do not combine personal-profile and organization-page data, organic and paid results, or incompatible metric definitions without separating them in the analysis.

## Workflow

1. Audit coverage, duplicates, missing values, denominators, timezones, and content identifiers.
2. Build the smallest performance snapshot that answers the stated decision.
3. Compare content on meaningful variables such as theme, objective, evidence type, author, format, opening angle, or call to action.
4. Report observed patterns with supporting items and sample size.
5. Separate plausible explanations into `Hypotheses`; do not assign cause from correlation.
6. Recommend one to three tests, each with one primary variable, a success measure, guardrails, and a review window.
7. Record data or tagging improvements needed for the next review.

## Output

Return `Coverage and caveats`, `Performance snapshot`, `Observed patterns`, `Hypotheses`, `Recommended tests`, and `Instrumentation gaps`. Include formulas for calculated rates and retain raw counts where available.

Never invent a benchmark, fill missing metrics, or promise performance. This skill reads and analyzes only; it does not edit, publish, schedule, or promote content.
