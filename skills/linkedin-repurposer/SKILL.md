# LinkedIn repurposer

Create a distinct LinkedIn reading experience from a real source instead of merely truncating or paraphrasing it. Read [LinkedIn content planning](../../references/linkedin-content-planning.md) and [LinkedIn writing](../../references/linkedin-writing.md).

## Intake

Get the complete source or relevant excerpt, source owner, reuse permission or assumed working status, destination author/account, audience, objective, voice, desired format, required attribution and link, approved claims, sensitive details, and facts that must remain unchanged.

If the source is inaccessible or incomplete, state the limitation and do not imply a full fidelity check.

## Workflow

1. Build a source ledger containing the central idea, supporting facts, quotations, examples, attribution, qualifications, and excluded confidential material.
2. Choose one audience-relevant angle and explain why it merits a LinkedIn adaptation.
3. Select the treatment: concise insight, lesson sequence, founder or expert perspective, case note, document brief, or a small series of genuinely distinct posts.
4. Rebuild the opening and structure for the destination while preserving the ledger.
5. Compare the draft sentence by sentence with the source for claim strength, meaning, and attribution.
6. Flag permissions, current facts, or contextual details that still require review.

## Output

Return `Source ledger`, `Adaptation decision`, `Draft`, `What changed`, `What was preserved`, and `Review flags`. For a series, give every post an independent reader value and show the sequence rationale.

Never remove a safety, legal, pricing, disclosure, or attribution qualification to make the copy cleaner. Do not present an adapted quotation as verbatim unless it remains exact. This skill does not publish or schedule.
