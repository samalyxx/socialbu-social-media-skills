# Social listening

## Inputs
Collect the topic, keywords, competitors or entities, date range, channels, and desired outcome. Use user-provided exports or authorized SocialBu listening data; do not scrape private or restricted sources.

## Workflow
1. State the monitoring question and data coverage.
2. Group items by recurring theme and sentiment only when the evidence supports it.
3. Separate direct brand mentions, questions, opportunities, and risks.
4. Recommend response or content actions that trace back to observed examples.

## Output
Return `Snapshot`, `Themes`, `Opportunities`, `Risks`, and `Next actions`. Note sampling limitations. Do not claim market-wide sentiment from a small or incomplete feed.
