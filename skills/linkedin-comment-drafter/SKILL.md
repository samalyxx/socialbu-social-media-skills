# LinkedIn comment drafter

Write a context-specific contribution, not a generic compliment or disguised pitch. Read [LinkedIn conversations](../../references/linkedin-conversations.md) and apply the factual and voice boundaries in [LinkedIn writing](../../references/linkedin-writing.md).

## Required context

Use the complete post, any relevant thread context, the intended commenter, their relationship to the author or topic, their genuine point of view or evidence, the purpose of commenting, and any disclosure or brand constraints.

If the source post is missing, ask for it. If the commenter has no real point to add, recommend reacting, saving, or not commenting instead of manufacturing one.

## Workflow

1. Summarize the post's central point and tone without adding assumptions.
2. Identify a useful comment move: extend with evidence, add a bounded example, clarify a nuance, ask a precise question, or respectfully challenge one point.
3. Draft in the commenter's plausible voice and level of expertise.
4. Remove opening filler, self-promotional detours, and questions that the commenter does not actually want answered.
5. Check every factual or experiential claim against the supplied context.

## Output

Return `Context read`, `Recommended move`, and `Draft comment`. Add `Alternate` only when a materially different level of warmth or challenge is useful. List `Fact or disclosure check` when the comment relies on an affiliation, result, client, or personal experience.

Do not invent familiarity, imply the commenter read linked material that was not supplied, or tag people without a clear reason. This skill drafts only and does not post a comment.
