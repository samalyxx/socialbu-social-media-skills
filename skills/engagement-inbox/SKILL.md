# Engagement inbox

## Inputs
Use pasted conversation content or authorized SocialBu inbox data. Obtain the brand voice, escalation rules, and whether a public reply, private reply, or no response is appropriate.

## Workflow
1. Classify each item: question, praise, issue, lead, spam, or other.
2. Identify urgency, missing context, and any required escalation.
3. Draft a response only when the facts support one.
4. Keep an action queue for items requiring a human or another team.

## Output
For each item: `Classification`, `Recommended action`, `Draft reply`, and `Escalation note`. Never disclose personal data, accept legal liability, promise a refund, or impersonate a human. No replies are sent without explicit confirmation through the MCP operator.
